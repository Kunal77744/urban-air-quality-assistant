# 2-3 Minute Video Demo Script

**Project**: Urban Air Quality Assistant  
**Audience**: AI Competition Judges & Evaluators

---

### Shot 1: Problem & Instant 1-Click Planning (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual**: Show `http://localhost:8000` opening on desktop browser.
- **Action**: Highlight that the plan loads immediately on page visit without requiring signups or tedious forms. Show the live AQI card and the AQI numerical index (not just color).
- **Narration**: *"Urban air pollution is a critical health crisis, yet existing tools only display numbers without actionable lifestyle solutions. Here is the Urban Air Quality Assistant: with a single click, it provides tailored daily routines, indoor air hacks, and verified emission impacts."*

### Shot 2: Lifestyle Tailoring & Sustainable Alternatives (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Action**: Switch commute mode from Motorcycle to Car, and observe instant reactive recalculation of CO2 savings.
- **Narration**: *"Notice that when I adjust commute distance or transit mode, the application dynamically recomputes our personal impact. Each suggestion provides a clear 'Why' line explaining how shifting to metro or bus reduces direct street-level tailpipe inhalation."*

### Shot 3: Indoor Air Hacks & Myth-Busting (1:00 - 1:30)
- **Action**: Scroll down to the Indoor Hacks section. Highlight the DIY Corsi-Rosenthal box, range hood ventilation, and the Plant Myth-Buster callout.
- **Narration**: *"For indoor protection, the assistant advises afternoon cross-ventilation when surface PM2.5 is lowest, explains how a ₹2,500 DIY filter achieves 90% particle capture, and debunks the common myth that houseplants alone can purify indoor particulate pollution."*

### Shot 4: Deterministic Impact Math & Zero-API Fallback (1:30 - 2:00)
- **Action**: Hover over the Impact card showing grams of CO2 saved and exposure reduction %.
- **Narration**: *"A core engineering breakthrough here is deterministic accountability: all emission reductions are calculated using peer-reviewed ARAI and WHO formulas. The LLM only enriches tone; it never hallucinates numbers, and the app works 100% offline without requiring any API keys."*

### Shot 5: Accessibility, Mobile Responsiveness & Hindi Toggle (2:00 - 2:30)
- **Action**: Click the 'हिंदी में देखें' toggle button to show instant bilingual translation. Resize browser down to 320px mobile width and navigate purely using the `Tab` keyboard key.
- **Narration**: *"Finally, the UI is built to WCAG 2.2 AA standards with full keyboard focus rings, screen reader announcements via ARIA live regions, seamless English/Hindi localization, and full responsiveness down to 320px width."*
